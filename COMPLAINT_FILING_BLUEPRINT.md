**Jurisdiction:** False advertising, deceptive trade practices, consumer fraud  
**Contact:** dncrp.gov.bd | Hotline: **16121** | TCB Bhaban, Karwan Bazar, Dhaka

**Evidence Package for DNCRP:**

```
Required:
□ Screenshots of Facebook posts claiming specific visa success rates
  (with archive.today timestamp URL)

□ Written statement from at least 1 victim (fee paid, service not rendered)
□ Your own documented chat requesting Mushak invoice (refused)

Optional but strengthening:
□ Email from Korean university denying partnership
□ Comparison: their claimed success rate vs. Embassy rejection statistics
```

**How to file:**

```
Option A — Online: dncrp.gov.bd → "অভিযোগ দাখিল" (File Complaint)
Option B — In Person: TCB Bhaban, Karwan Bazar, Dhaka (Mon–Thu, 9am–4pm)
Option C — Hotline: Call 16121

In your complaint state:
"[Company] is making unsubstantiated claims of [X]% visa success rate
in Facebook advertising (evidence attached) without documentary proof,
constituting a deceptive trade practice under Section 2(20) of the
Consumer Rights Protection Act 2009."
```
